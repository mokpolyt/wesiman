RED ROBOT RUN

Reach the flag at the end of the level. Shoot the soldiers and grab the floating ? boxes for a weapon (spread gun, axe, uzi), then use THROW to fire it. Press JUMP twice to float for 6 seconds (once every 30 seconds). If you fall in battle you respawn from the sky. When your chest glows (every 9 to 15 seconds), tap SHOOT twice for a huge red laser.

Keys: arrows or A/D to move, Space or W to jump, Z or K to shoot, J or C to throw box weapon
